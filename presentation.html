THE CHALLENGE

Why Modern Travel Planning is Broken

Travelers face extreme fragmentation, generic commercial traps, and regional safety barriers.

❌ Planning Fatigue & 20+ Open Tabs

Travelers spend 10–15 hours toggling between blogs, map apps, transport schedules, and booking engines for a single 3-day vacation.

❌ Impersonal & Crowded Hotspots

Search engines prioritize sponsored tourist traps while authentic local culture, folklore, and hidden architectural gems remain undiscovered.

❌ Language Barriers & Transit Confusion

Navigating local metro, buses, and regional Indian dialects creates anxiety and unexpected transit expenses.

❌ Safety Blindspots

Lack of instant emergency SOS access, weather warnings, and local helplines when visiting unfamiliar areas.

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

Modern Full-Stack Engineering

High-throughput, decoupled architecture built for sub-second performance.

🎨 Frontend (React 18 + Vite)

  • Modular Code-Splitting: 87% bundle reduction via React.lazy & Suspense (76 kB main bundle).
  • Design System: Bengali Heritage palette (Terracotta #c85a44, Saffron #e8a048, Parchment).
  • Interactive Mapping: Leaflet & OpenStreetMap with 0ms in-memory landmark geocoder.
  • State & Interceptors: Axios interceptors with automatic 401 token refresh/redirect.

⚙️ Backend (Node.js + Express)

  • Database Layer: MongoDB Atlas with multi-DNS SRV fallback (Google DNS 8.8.8.8).
  • Auth & Security: Passport.js Google OAuth 2.0 + 10-round salted bcrypt + JWT.
  • AI Integration: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash API with resilient fallback cascade.
  • Production Hardening: Helmet headers, strict CORS, and rate limiting.
